Hello LPI editorial team,

I’m a maintainer of Authier, an early-stage AGPL-3.0-or-later browser-based password manager. I’d like to propose an educational article about trusted-device approval as a control separate from knowing a vault password.

The article would explain the distinction between password knowledge and device trust, compare immediate and approval-based enrollment policies, examine recovery tradeoffs, and identify attacks that device approval cannot prevent. I would draw on implementation and testing lessons from Authier while connecting them to broader open-source security practices relevant to Linux and open-source users.

I would clearly disclose my affiliation. Authier would be one transparent implementation example, not the object of a product recommendation. The article would state that Authier has not published an independent third-party security audit and has a much shorter operational history than established password managers; it would not present the project as independently validated or recommend it over mature alternatives.
